# Recurring mistakes (from my own lessons, Sep 2026)

Watch for these. Name one only when the position actually shows the flag; don't force them.

- **Loose pieces.** I move without scanning for anything hanging, mine or theirs. Before a move:
  what's undefended, and what does this move (or chasing that piece) stop defending?
- **Forcing first.** I grab material or develop calmly instead of looking for a check or a capture
  with tempo first. Before taking a pawn: can they win it back with tempo?
- **Gambit safety (Vienna / f4 gambits).** I forget the h4-e1 diagonal: pawn off f2 plus king on e1
  means ...Qh4+. Cover h4 (Nf3) first, and know my plan if the gambit is accepted and if it's declined.
- **Reading the opponent.** I choose my plan before working out which way his king is castling and
  what his last pawn move is fighting for.
- **Cramped c8-bishop.** In d4 and fianchetto positions I play ...e6 before developing it.
- **Converting.** When a pawn up I keep pushing it instead of trading pieces, especially the one
  that could win it back.
- **Checks.** When in check I don't list move, block and capture, or check each king square for mate.

Lines come from master games, every move is checked by the engine, and the coach writes the notes. A lesson is built once (a few minutes, a few cents), then learning and drilling it is free.

♔ Endgame trainer

Every drill is a fresh random position. Up to 7 pieces the result is exact (Lichess tablebase) and each move you make is checked; bigger pawn set-ups use Stockfish. You play against full-strength Stockfish with the eval bar off.
